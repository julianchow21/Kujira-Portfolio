Refunds draft

A factual note about the current preview and the payment decisions still awaiting review.

Current preview

This preview has no enabled checkout and publishes no price or paid purchase flow. It therefore does not publish a refund process for a purchase made through this preview.

The Pro plan is shown as a planned direction with pricing not set. Checkout is unavailable in this preview.

Future payment terms awaiting review

If a paid plan is enabled later, its cancellation, renewal, payment support and refund process need to be written and published before purchase.

No refund outcome, time limit, renewal rule or payment obligation is set by this draft.

Owner and legal review items

The following points remain open and are not settled by this draft:

  • operator identity and payment support contact
  • available payment methods and any billing provider flow
  • cancellation, renewal and access-after-cancellation behaviour
  • refund eligibility, process and any applicable time periods
  • jurisdictional scope and service terms

These items await owner and legal review. This page is not legal advice.
