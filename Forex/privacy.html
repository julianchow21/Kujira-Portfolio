Privacy draft

A factual outline of the current local preview and the privacy decisions still awaiting review.

Current local preview

The local journal can keep journal entries, presentation settings and other values needed by the local experience in browser storage on this device. The browser controls can be used to remove local site data.

This preview does not submit a sign-up form or payment. Online accounts, hosted sync and billing are not available in this preview.

Hosted scope awaiting review

No hosted collection or account policy is approved by this draft. If hosted features are enabled later, their data flows and user controls need to be documented before launch.

The future review needs to identify the data categories, service providers, access boundaries and user actions that apply to hosted accounts.

Owner and legal review items

The following points remain open and are not settled by this draft:

  • operator identity, privacy contact and support route
  • hosted data categories, providers and processing roles
  • retention, deletion, export and account closure behaviour
  • security controls, cookies or similar technologies
  • jurisdictional scope and any special data handling terms

These items await owner and legal review. This page is not legal advice.
