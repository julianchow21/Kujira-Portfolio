Structure

The biggest lift. Today every view competes for the same top bar and the dashboard repeats itself.

Now11 tabs, the app switcher, currency, sync pill and four icon buttons share one bar. At 1440px wide Insurance is cut to "I", and the overflow into More changes from tab to tab.
ProposedA left sidebar grouped as Overview, Assets and Money. The top bar keeps page-level controls only: search, currency, hide balances, Add.
NowAllocation appears four times on the dashboard: the asset cards, the stacked bar, the target bars and the doughnut. Five amber banners then restate the target bars.
ProposedOne "Where it sits" card: a single split bar, one row per class with a target marker, and one plain-language insight.
NowThe Chart Builder sits at the bottom of the dashboard. Holdings open an Edit modal.
ProposedChart Builder moves to its own Explore page. A holding opens a side panel with price, position, dividends and trades, and Edit lives inside it.

Visual system

Premium mostly comes from restraint and consistent numbers.

  • One typeface with tabular figures. Lexend headings, system body text and SF Mono table numbers currently mix. Monospace digits make the tables read like a developer console. I used Manrope here. Google Fonts is already in your CSP, so the swap needs no CSP change.
  • Big numbers go light, not heavy. The headline figure in weight 300 with a small, dim currency prefix looks like a private-banking statement. Weight 800 looks like a dashboard template.
  • Always show the currency. Dashboard shows "$679,740" while Stocks shows "US$41,638", so you can't tell the base at a glance. Use S$ and US$ everywhere, styled smaller and dimmer than the digits.
  • Colour has jobs. Teal is for brand and primary actions only. Gains and losses get their own green and red, and each asset class gets a distinct hue. Stocks and Crypto are both teal today, and gains use a stock Material green that clashes with the accent.
  • Fewer boxes and less decoration. Drop the coloured rails on KPI cards, the background glow, emoji empty states (👀 📊 📅) and the per-row chips (US, SGX, Deposit, Contribution). Market goes as quiet text under the ticker. Your CSP blocks external logo images, so use locally generated monograms like the ones above.
  • One quiet status line. Local only, Books balanced, CPF off and Prices 31s ago are four separate pills now. Merge them into the line under the page title, and move captions like "X: Asset Class · Y: Value · Top all · doughnut" and the FIFO footnote into info tooltips.

Interactions that feel premium

Small, consistent motion and direct manipulation. All of these are in the mockup.

  • Scrub the chart and the headline number, date and change follow your pointer, the way Robinhood and Apple Stocks do. The line takes the colour of the period's result.
  • Hide balances with one tap. Amounts blur, percentages stay readable. Handy on the MRT or when screen sharing.
  • Prices that change flash green or red for a moment on refresh, and headline figures roll to new values instead of jumping.
  • ⌘K command bar to jump to any page, add a trade or look up a ticker.
  • Skeleton placeholders while prices load, and Undo on toasts instead of confirm dialogs.

Mobile

  • Holdings as list rows showing ticker, value and return. On a 375px phone the P&L columns currently sit off-screen in a sideways-scrolling table.
  • Filters as one scrolling chip row with actions behind a single Add button. The Stocks toolbar wraps to three rows of buttons today.
  • Bottom bar of five areas (Overview, Assets, Money, Plan, More) matching the desktop sidebar groups, so both layouts share one mental map.

Bugs I noticed along the way

  • The Crypto card on the dashboard shows an empty teal square where its icon should be, in both themes.
  • At 375px wide, pages with the SGD/USD toggle lay out at 395px. The top bar actions overflow and the settings gear is clipped.
  • The net worth chart axis shows ISO dates (2025-11-01) and bare numbers (570,000) with no currency.

Order I'd ship it in

The first three are mostly CSS and token changes, and give most of the visible lift. Later items change structure and need the usual QA pass on every tab.
